# Exhaust the design space

When a UI interaction or architectural decision has no precedent in the codebase, build two or three competing prototypes before committing.

Compare alternatives against the same question and constraints. Use the comparison to choose a direction, rather than treating the first plausible solution as the answer.

Apply this principle to product decisions where the experience depends on how an interaction feels, not just whether its logic works.
